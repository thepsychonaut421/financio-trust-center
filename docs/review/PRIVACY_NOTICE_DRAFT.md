# Financio Trust Center — Privacy Notice

DRAFT FOR REVIEW — version 2026-10-11. Publication and retention decisions pending.

## Scope and controller

This notice covers the Financio Trust Center at https://trust.financioflow.com,
including document requests, access links and administration. The controller is
Rares-Daniel Balauta, sole proprietor (Einzelunternehmen), Albrechtsgasse 17,
94315 Straubing, Germany. Privacy requests: info@financioflow.com. Provider
identification is available at https://financioflow.com/impressum.

Use of the ERP is covered by its own policies and applicable customer agreements.
Requesting Trust Center documents does not create an ERP account or authorize
processing of customer accounting records through this portal.

## Data and purposes

When you request documents, we process the name, email address, company,
requested document identifiers and any message you provide. We use them to
review the request, correspond with you and deliver approved access. Please do
not include unnecessary financial, health or other sensitive personal data.

We retain the review decision, reviewer, timestamps, link expiry and access
status. Access links are bearer credentials: anyone who obtains an active link
may use its approved permissions. Keep them confidential. We record first use
and document downloads for security and access administration. Where an NDA is
required, the acceptance record includes the request, email, agreement URL,
time, IP address and user-agent information.

Administration additionally uses an authorized account's identity, authentication
credentials, sessions and activity records. The authentication provider handles
password verification and recovery. Infrastructure and email providers process
technical connection, security and delivery information needed to provide their
services. Exact provider retention and account controls require confirmation
before this draft is approved.

## Legal bases

The proposed bases are steps connected with a prospective or existing business
relationship where Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies, and legitimate interests under
Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in answering business security enquiries, controlling
confidential disclosures, preventing misuse and retaining evidence of review
and acceptance. For employees acting for another organization, legitimate
interests may be the applicable basis rather than a personal contract.

Legal obligations may require specified processing under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.
Where a legitimate-interest basis applies, you may object on grounds relating to
your situation. An NDA checkbox records agreement to confidentiality terms;
it is not blanket consent to all personal-data processing or marketing.

## Recipients and locations

Authorized operators receive request and review information. Providers currently
used for this portal include Supabase for the database and administrator
authentication, Cloudflare for the public tunnel and edge security, Resend for
transactional email, Proton for the operator mailbox, and GitHub for code hosting,
CI and source evidence. Our dedicated application host stores uploaded documents
and operational backups; an operator workstation holds encrypted backup copies.

The Supabase project is configured in Frankfurt. The Resend sending domain uses
its Ireland region. These settings are not a guarantee that every provider
processes all data exclusively in the EEA. The service-provider register must
identify the account contracts, relevant processing locations and applicable
Chapter V GDPR safeguards, such as an appropriate adequacy decision or standard
contractual clauses, before this notice is approved. No provider certification
is asserted as our own.

## Retention

The proposed operational schedule is to review pending or denied requests for
deletion after 90 days, approved request/access records after 12 months following
expiry, and NDA acceptance evidence after the period necessary for the agreement
and legitimate legal claims. Legal holds can require longer retention of specific
records. These proposed targets need operator approval and implementation.

The current pilot does not automatically purge these records or encrypted
backups. Daily backup creation and copying are configured; deletion and provider
retention follow their separately verified procedures. Revoking a link prevents
future access but does not itself delete its audit record, a provider's delivery
log or an earlier downloaded copy. We will assess verified deletion requests
individually, including applicable exceptions and backup handling.

## Cookies, automation and choices

Administrator authentication uses necessary session storage/cookies. The portal
uses review decisions to grant access; a matching organization domain does not
automatically approve a request. Draft security updates may be prepared from
source and CI evidence, but an administrator decides what to publish. The
current draft generator does not call an external AI model. No Salesforce
integration is enabled for this deployment.

A document request is voluntary. Required fields are needed to review and deliver
that request; without them, access cannot be processed. Optional message text
is not required. This pilot does not intentionally configure an advertising
product around request data. Provider-specific cookies, security controls and
email tracking settings must be checked as part of the final register review.

## Your rights and contact

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion,
restriction and data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate
interests. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for the future.
Contact info@financioflow.com; we may request proportionate identity verification
before disclosing data or changing access.

You may complain to a competent supervisory authority. For a private-sector
operator in Bavaria, the Bayerisches Landesamt fuer Datenschutzaufsicht
(BayLDA), https://www.lda.bayern.de, is an applicable authority. You may also
contact the authority available under Article 77 GDPR for your situation.

We will identify the effective date and publish substantive changes when this
notice is approved. An approved version will replace this working draft.
