# Financio Trust Center — Internal Document Access Policy

DRAFT FOR OPERATOR APPROVAL — version 2026-10-11.

## Responsibilities and review

The proposed owner is the operator identified in the Impressum. The initial
administrator account is info@financioflow.com. Security enquiries are received
at security@financioflow.com. Only designated administrators may upload,
publish, review access requests or revoke access.

Before publishing, review provenance, scope, dates, confidentiality, personal
information, vendor redistribution rights and intended audience. A working
policy is not an independent audit report. Do not create certification entries
without authentic evidence of issuer, scope and validity.

Save new material as a draft. File replacement returns the document to draft.
For NDA-protected material, approve a versioned, immutable NDA URL first and
keep the document restricted. Never change NDA wording behind the same URL.

## Requests and delivery

Check the requester's business purpose, identity/contact and authority. A
matching email domain or an earlier organization request is insufficient by
itself. Make an explicit decision for each request or selected batch. If the
organization is inactive or blocked, resolve that status before approval.

Links expire after at most seven days; a shorter requested approval period
shortens the link. Delivery is to the stated request email. Avoid sharing links
in public chat, logs or tickets. Required NDA acceptance occurs through the
approved link and is associated with that request and agreement URL.

If delivery fails, the saved approval is visible to the administrator. Do not
blindly approve again or send repeated messages. Check the delivery dashboard,
revoke the request where needed and arrange a new request deliberately.

Revocation removes the current bearer link. It stops future requests to the
access/download endpoints, but cannot remove copies already obtained. Withdrawn,
expired or draft documents are unavailable even through an older approved link.

## Retention and recovery

Approve and implement a request/audit/acceptance retention schedule. Proposed
review targets are in the privacy draft. No automatic deletion is currently
asserted. Record legal holds separately and minimize retained personal data.
Review encrypted backup retention explicitly; backup creation is not deletion.

Use the administrator recovery flow and the existing encrypted-backup runbook.
Maintain the recovery identity outside the application VM. Record exercises and
failures without preserving live passwords or bearer tokens in evidence files.

## Publication decisions

A CI success is supporting evidence, not proof of deployment or Security Cloud
finding closure. Public changelog wording must be approved against its specific
source revision and current evidence. AI assistance may draft text; it must not
publish, approve access or certify controls autonomously.
