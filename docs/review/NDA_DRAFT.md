## Parties and purpose

Financio Flow. Contact: info@financioflow.com.

The recipient is the person or organization identified in the approved document

privacy and suitability of Financio Flow for a prospective or existing business
relationship.

## Confidential information

This agreement covers non-public reports and documents supplied through an
approved Trust Center access request and identified as confidential, together
with non-public information whose confidential nature is reasonably apparent.
Publicly available material is not made confidential merely by appearing in the
Trust Center.

It does not cover information the recipient can demonstrate was already lawfully
known, becomes public without a breach, is received lawfully without a
confidentiality duty, or is independently developed without using the disclosed
information.

## Use and protection

The recipient will use confidential information only for the permitted purpose,
protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to employees or professional
advisers who need it for that purpose and are bound by suitable confidentiality
obligations. The recipient remains responsible for disclosures by people to whom
it provides the information, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Do not share access links or publish non-public reports without written
permission. No intellectual property license is granted beyond the limited right
to review the supplied information for the permitted purpose. This agreement
does not authorize security testing of any system.

## Required and protected disclosures

A disclosure required by law, court order or a competent authority is permitted.
Where lawful, the recipient will notify the disclosing party before disclosure,
limit the disclosure to what is required and cooperate with reasonable requests
to protect confidentiality. Nothing restricts legally protected reporting,
whistleblowing, communications with regulators or the exercise of mandatory
statutory rights.

## Duration and return

The proposed confidentiality period is three years from each disclosure. For
information protected as a trade secret under applicable law, the duty continues
while that information qualifies for that protection.

At the disclosing party's reasonable written request, the recipient will cease
use and delete or return confidential copies, except records it must retain by
law and routine backup copies not reasonably separable. Retained copies remain
protected and must not be used for another purpose.

## Reports and other agreements

Documents describe the scope and dates stated in them. They are not a warranty
of future security, a certification or a service level agreement. This agreement
does not replace a separate signed NDA that expressly governs the disclosure;
that signed agreement takes precedence to the extent of a conflict.

## Law and acceptance

German law applies, subject to mandatory applicable rights. Jurisdiction follows
applicable law; this draft does not impose an exclusive court on every recipient.

Electronic acceptance records the approved request, the versioned agreement URL,
acceptance time, and technical audit information described in the privacy notice.
The agreement does not require consent to optional marketing.
